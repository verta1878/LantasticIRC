
                       {Artisoft Technical Support
                       {==========================

       Artisoft offers individualized technical support for all of its
       products. Outside the United States, please contact your Artisoft
       representative.

    {Calling Technical Support
    {-------------------------

       Artisoft Technical Support is available Monday through Friday from
       7:00 a.m. through 5:00 p.m. Mountain Standard Time, excluding
       holidays. For express response in the domestic United States, call
       1 (900) 555-TECH (555-8324). The rate is $2.50 per minute. If you
       don't have a touch-tone phone, stay on the line to reach an
       Artisoft operator, who will direct your call to the Artisoft
       Technical Support Phone group.

       During the same hours of operation, Artisoft also offers free
       Technical Support at 1 (602) 670-7000.

       Before calling Artisoft Technical Support, please take a moment to
       collect the following information.

         - The version of your LANtastic program and the type of network
           adapter being used – for example, NodeRunner 2000/C.

         - The versions of DOS and Windows running on your computer and a
           description of the hardware involved.

         - The name and model number of your computer.

       In addition, complete the following steps:

         - Write down any error messages you've received, and a brief
           description of the problem.

         - Put your phone as close to the computer as possible.

         - Have your network adapter instructions handy, along with this
           manual.

         - Write down the serial number from the label of Disk Number 1 of
           your LANtastic diskettes.

    {Arti-Facts Electronic Bulletin Board
    {------------------------------------

       Artisoft maintains its own 24-hour Bulletin Board Service, the
       Arti-Facts BBS, that is available for all registered users of
       Artisoft products. The BBS number is 1 (602) 884-8648. The
       communication settings for the Arti-Facts BBS appear below:

         - 1200 – 9600 baud (V.32) or 38,400 baud (US Robotics HST modems)
         - 8 data bits
         - 1 stop bit
         - No parity
         - Full duplex

       Files available for downloading include:

         - Compatibility bulletins that describe how to install other
           manufacturers' software with LANtastic.

         - Software programs such as LAN Radio that include networking
           features.

    {Arti-Facts BBS Tips
    {-------------------

         - When you dial up to the BBS, you're asked to enter your first
           and last name. If this is your first dial-up session and you're
           prompted for a password, try entering your name in a different
           way – for example, Thomas Stone instead of Tom Stone.

         - If you press Enter at the prompt to scan the message base, you
           can select and read messages from other LANtastic users.

         - The message base is also available from the Arti-Facts main
           menu, along with many other information resources. For help
           with any command on this menu, select the command and press the
           H key.

         - To get the most from your first dial-up session, download the
           following files:

           BLTS.ZIP, which gives you all technical bulletins in one
           compressed file.

           BBSLIST.TXT, which lists all files that are available from
           the BBS.

    {Artisoft FaxReturn
    {------------------

       Artisoft maintains an electronic facsimile system, the Artisoft
       FaxReturn(SM) system. This menu-driven system automatically
       downloads current technical bulletins and sales information to your
       fax machine. To use this service, call 1 (602) 884-1397 from the
       phone attached to your fax. Listen to the menu and choose up to
       three faxes per call. After you've selected your faxes, wait for
       instructions on how to receive them.
