
{                Installing and Using LANtastic with NDIS Drivers
{                ================================================

       LANtastic NDIS support lets you use Artisoft or third-party network
       adapters and NDIS drivers with LANtastic.  Supported adapter types
       include Ethernet, Token-Ring and ARCNET(R) adapters.  To function
       with LANtastic, your adapter must have an NDIS MAC (Media Access
       Control) driver.

       {Note} For Token-Ring installations, refer to the {Source Routing
              {Support with IBM Token-Ring Networks} section in the
              LANtastic NDIS Support menu of this on-line manual.

       To use an adapter with an NDIS driver in your LANtastic network:

         1. Install the adapter in your PC as instructed in your adapter
            documentation.

         2. Install LANtastic as instructed in your LANtastic manuals.
            When asked what type of adapter you're using, select "NDIS
            Support for Network Adapters" and then continue with the
            installation.

         3. When prompted, place the diskette containing the NDIS driver
            for your adapter into a floppy drive and type in the drive
            letter.  This diskette is usually provided by the adapter
            manufacturer.

            You may also need to enter the directory where the NDIS
            software is located on the driver diskette.  See your adapter
            documentation for more information.

         4. Proceed with the installation as instructed in your LANtastic
            Manuals.

       If you have problems with your installation, see the
       {Troubleshooting} section in the LANtastic NDIS Support menu of
       this on-line manual.
